# Guide: lessons

## Rules
- Lessons are general rules earlier cases taught us, each approved by the owner. Read them before a job and follow them as you follow any rule here.
- A lesson never overrides the person's answer, consent, leakage or a limit.
- After a case is ready, you may `propose_lesson` once or twice: one general sentence, no numbers, no values from the data, no names. The owner decides; do not assume it is kept.
- Propose only what would have saved a step or a question. Skip what is only true of this file.
