# The customer pitch (the UI must make every line true and visible)

Autonomy: your always-on marketing team, paid in results

The problem you already know: you send the same "10% off" email to everyone who hasn't bought in 60 days. Some of them would have bought anyway, so you just gave away margin. Some ignore you. Some get annoyed and unsubscribe. And at the end of the month, nobody can tell you whether the campaign actually made money.

What Autonomy does: it looks after every customer one by one, every day, and asks one question: "What's the most profitable thing to do for this person right now, including doing nothing?" Then it acts, watches what happens, learns, and shows you the money it actually made for you.

How it works for you: five simple screens
1. Connect. Upload your customer and order files, or connect your database. Our Data Doctor cleans up messy data and asks before changing anything.
2. Goal. Type what you want, in plain words: "Win back customers who stopped buying, $5,000 this month." Or pick one of the opportunities it found for you: "1,900 customers are drifting away. Want me to handle them?"
3. Plan. It shows you its plan in plain English: who it will contact, which offers it may use, which channels, at most how often, quiet hours and budget. It also shows you the actual messages it wrote. You approve once.
4. Live. A daily briefing: "Yesterday I contacted 341 customers, left 1,200 alone, and here's why." Tap any customer and see every decision with a reason: "Sent Priya a reminder with no discount: she always buys at full price, and email is cheapest."
5. Proof. How much extra profit it made compared with a group it never contacted, with an honest range. If it isn't sure yet, it says so.

What makes it different
- It doesn't waste your discounts. Most tools target people "likely to buy". Autonomy targets people whose behaviour it can actually change. Loyal buyers get a reminder, not a coupon. Price-sensitive shoppers get an offer only when it pays for itself.
- It knows when to stay quiet. Every message has a cost: annoyance, unsubscribes, spam complaints. Autonomy weighs that cost every time, so "do nothing" often wins. Less spam, healthier lists.
- It picks the whole combination for each person. Who, when, which channel, which offer, which message, which image, and how often. Channels: email and WhatsApp today; SMS and your own tools through a hand-off. Timing: it learns when each person actually reads their messages.
- It proves every rupee, or dollar. A small random group of customers is never contacted. Autonomy compares everyone else against them, so you see real extra profit, not "attributed revenue". It also measures itself against a simple rule anyone could set up, so you know the AI is earning its keep.
- You stay in control. Shadow: it decides and shows you, but sends nothing. Approve: you approve each batch with one click. Autopilot: it acts on its own inside the limits you set. Hard rules it can never break: consent, quiet hours, weekly limits, budget, no promotions after a complaint. It auto-pauses itself if complaints or unsubscribes spike. One big Pause button.
- It writes on-brand messages, safely. An AI copywriter drafts the messages and a second AI acts as a strict editor: brand voice, no false claims, no made-up numbers, unsubscribe line present, channel rules followed. Nothing goes out without passing.
- It knows your products. It predicts when each customer is due to reorder, and suggests complementary products. On real shop data, its suggestions got about 58% more purchases than just recommending best-sellers.

A day with Autonomy
8:55 am. Priya bought a serum 42 days ago and has looked at moisturisers twice. Autonomy predicts she's due for a refill. She's always bought at full price and reads WhatsApp in the evening. Decision: a refill reminder by WhatsApp at 7 pm, mentioning the moisturiser she viewed. No discount. Meanwhile: 1,200 other customers are left alone today. Some were contacted twice this week, some are better off without a message, and one has an open complaint. Next morning: "Yesterday: 341 messages, 24 purchases. Extra profit vs the untouched group so far: $570 (range $310-$830)."

- When the proof page shows real extra profit: switch to Autopilot.
