# CMO review: a senior buyer's evaluation (summary)
Verdict: "Sell the honesty now and earn the autonomy." Would not buy the autonomous brain today; would run a lean, measurement-first pilot.
Strong: proof built in (80/10/10, ranges, 'not sure yet'); radical honesty (published losses, pre-registered targets); profit-first, margin floor, do-nothing; control design (shadow/approve/autopilot, hard rules, auto-pauses, signed mandates); explained decisions; plain-English UI; India groundwork (DLT, DND, templates, festival calendar); team fixes what it measures (Hillstrom calibration 11x -> 1.46x; MegaFon beats simple rule).
Worries (ranked): 1 no real-business result, ties a simple rule at best on real data; 2 restraint is done by caps not intelligence (top leave-alone reason 'weekly limit reached'; hero customer got 4 messages in 9 days); 3 demo shows unearned numbers (111.6x ROI on a simulated shop with no 'simulated' label, spend mismatch $24.14 vs $17.25, $1 suggested budget, follow-ups shown but never sent, briefing lines the engine cannot produce, cherry-picked 58% recommender claim, editor gate enforced only in the UI); 4 data readiness is an engineering project (consent events, Shopify OAuth not ready, 20 MB CSV); 5 thin integration with Braze/MoEngage/CleverTap (webhook only; competing holdouts, caps blind to their sends); 6 India/enterprise compliance missing (DPA, DPDP processor posture, SOC 2/ISO, India residency, WhatsApp 131049 cap handling); 7 brand risk (bland template copy; v3 copy/images not wired; 'email autopilot from day one' contradicts 'start in shadow'); 8 org fit (20% of base held back; needs CFO/founder sponsor); 9 effectively a managed service; 10 no pricing; 11 vendor risk (team = founder + Claude; v3 built in a day, mostly off).
Pilot conditions: one win-back goal, email first, approve each batch 3 weeks, v3 off unless CI shows it wins; weeks 0-3 read-only audit + shadow; >=100k customers; three groups: agent / the client's own business-as-usual / 10% no-contact; 12 weeks + 6-8 week read; metric incremental gross margin per eligible customer vs BAU; buy if it beats BAU with lower bound > 0, or ties while cutting messages 30%+ and discounts 20%+, forecast within 1.5x, <3-4 h/week of team time.
Right first customer: Indian D2C/consumer brand, 100k-2M repeat-purchase customers, discount-heavy win-back, Shopify+Klaviyo or own email + WhatsApp Cloud API, clean opt-ins, no DS team, founder/CFO sponsor. Not for: enterprises on SFMC/Adobe, <50k shops, telcos/banks/subscriptions, brands without consent records, acquisition-led brands.
Five changes: 1 lead with proof not autonomy (audit + shadow as entry product, drop 'paid in results' until pricing exists); 2 strip the demo of unearned numbers and inconsistencies, enforce the editor gate on the server; 3 one real design partner with a live holdout, publish the readout, ship only v3 parts that beat the plain agent, fix restraint; 4 productised onboarding + read/write integration on top of MoEngage/CleverTap/WebEngage/Braze; 5 India/enterprise hygiene (DPA, residency, pseudonymised IDs, SOC 2 plan, WhatsApp cap/quality handling), pilots start in Approve, publish a price list.
