# Which platforms our customers use (connector market share)

Research date: 20 Oct 2026. Scope: SMB and mid-market, mostly B2C (e-commerce, D2C, subscriptions, apps, services), global.

**How to read this.** Numbers come from different trackers that measure different things: Store Leads counts live stores, BuiltWith and W3Techs count websites, filings count paying customers or ARR, and Ramp counts its own card customers. They are not comparable with each other, so use them as ranges. Each row marks the type: **Filing** (audited or regulatory filing), **Tracker** (third-party crawl), **Vendor** (company claim) or **Est.** (analyst or blog estimate). The page fetcher was down during this research, so figures were read from search-engine extracts of the linked pages. Re-check any number before quoting it outside the team.

Next wave, by region or segment: **Brevo** (Europe), **Recharge** (Shopify subscriptions; partly visible through Shopify), **Razorpay + MoEngage/CleverTap** (India pack), **Tiendanube** (LatAm), **RevenueCat** (apps), **Postscript/Attentive** (US SMS), **Wix**. Ad audiences (Meta, Google) are a separate question: they are execution channels, not data sources.

### Does 10 cover 80%?

Not globally. Our own estimate, defining "covered" as a company's main customer and order data source **plus** at least one send channel both being connectable:

| Segment / region | Estimated coverage with the top 10 | Main gaps |
|---|---|---|
| US/UK/AU e-com and D2C | **70–80%** | Wix/Squarespace micro-stores, BigCommerce, Magento, Attentive/Postscript-only SMS |
| Europe e-com | **55–65%** | Shopware (DE), PrestaShop (FR/ES), Brevo as the ESP |
| India D2C and apps | **35–50%** | Razorpay, MoEngage, CleverTap, WebEngage, BSP-held WhatsApp history, Dukaan |
| LatAm | **40–50%** | Tiendanube, VTEX, Mercado Shops, RD Station |
| Subscription apps | **30–45%** | RevenueCat, Braze, OneSignal, Firebase/Amplitude (partly reachable via the Segment spec or a warehouse) |
| Services SMBs | **45–55%** | Zoho, Salesforce, Pipedrive, ActiveCampaign, vertical tools (booking or POS systems) |
| **Blended global ICP** | **~50–60%** | |

Coverage rises to roughly **65–75%** if we also accept a universal fallback that does not count as a named connector: CSV/SFTP upload plus webhooks, so long-tail data still flows. Reaching 80% globally needs about 15–18 connectors, including a region pack for India (Razorpay, MoEngage or CleverTap) and one for Europe/LatAm (Brevo, Shopware, Tiendanube). If we launch US-first with Shopify-centric D2C, 10 connectors do get close to 80%.
