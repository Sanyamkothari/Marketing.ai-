# Participant information and consent form

> **Review with your legal adviser before use.** This form describes in plain English what the sessions in
> [`USABILITY_TEST.md`](../USABILITY_TEST.md) do with a participant's information, and is written with India's Digital
> Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in mind. It is a plain description of the practice in the kit, not legal advice and not
> a statement that the practice meets any law. Fill in every `[square bracket]`, have the text and the retention periods
> checked against your own obligations, and change the form if your practice differs. If the practice and the form disagree,
> change the practice or the form before the session, never during it.

Give the participant this form (printed, or by e-mail in advance, see [`RECRUITMENT.md`](RECRUITMENT.md)) and let them read
it before the session. Read the key points aloud at the start. They may ask questions first. There are two separate
choices below: taking part, and being recorded. Saying no to the recording does not stop the person taking part, and does
not change the thank-you.

---

## About this session

**Who is asking:** `[organisation name]`, `[address]` ("we"). We decide why and how the information below is used.

**What this is:** a test of a marketing tool we are building. You will be given two short tasks on cards and asked to use
the tool the way you would at work, thinking aloud as you go. **We are testing the tool, not you.** Nothing you do is a right
or wrong answer. The session takes about 45 minutes.

**Why we ask for information:** to find where the screens of the tool are hard to use, so that we can fix them, and to decide
whether a new layout of the tool is ready. We use it for nothing else.

## What we collect

1. **Details about you.** Your job title, years in marketing, the campaign tools you have used, and your answers to the
   booking questions. In our notes you are named P1 to P5 or by initials, **not** by name.
2. **Notes taken by an observer** about what you did, where you hesitated, what you said, how long each task took, and the
   ratings you give. These notes do not hold your name.
3. **A recording**, only if you agree below:
   - **the screen** while you use the tool, and
   - **your voice** as you think aloud, which is a separate choice.
4. **Your name and contact details,** used only to arrange the session and the thank-you (`[incentive]`), kept apart from
   the notes.

The tool in the session shows a **demonstration store of published research data with no names or contact details** (an e-mail marketing experiment that has
been published for research). It does not show your company's customers or your own information. Please do not type your own
customers' personal data into it. If something of that kind appears in a recording, tell us, and we will remove it.

## Your choices and rights

- **Taking part is voluntary.** You may refuse any question, and **stop at any time, without giving a reason**. If you
  stop early, you still receive the thank-you.
- **Recording is a separate choice** (below). You may say no to the screen recording, to the voice recording, or to both, and
  still take part. You may also ask us to stop the recording during the session.
- **You can withdraw your agreement at any time,** as easily as you gave it, by telling us on the day or by writing to the
  contact below. We will then stop using your information and delete what we hold about you: your details, your recording
  and the form. Notes that no longer say who you are cannot be traced back to you, so we may be unable to take your
  contribution out of notes we have already combined with other people's; we will tell you if that applies.
- **You can ask** to see the information we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to have it erased.
- **You can complain** to us first at the contact below. `[Name the person who handles complaints, as your legal adviser
  advises.]` The law also provides a Data Protection Board of India for complaints that are not resolved; your legal adviser
  can tell you how this applies.
- You must be **18 or over** to take part.

## For the facilitator

- Do not start the recorder until the person has agreed to it, and say aloud when it starts.
- If the person declines the recording, do not record. The observer writes notes, and the click counts and timings are taken
  by hand. Note on the observer's sheet that the session was not recorded.
- If the person withdraws on the day, stop, delete anything recorded, and do not count the session. Give the thank-you.
- Put the participant id on the form and on the results sheet; keep the name on the form only.
- After the notes are checked, delete the recording on the day you promised, and record that you did.
