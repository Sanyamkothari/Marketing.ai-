A customer replied to a marketing message. What do they want? Choose exactly one label.

- stop: they want no more messages (stop, unsubscribe, opt out, remove me, leave me alone).
- not_now: they are not interested at the moment but are not asking to stop (busy, later, maybe next month).
- question: they ask something about an order, a product, the offer, delivery or their account.
- complaint: they are unhappy with us or the message (spam, rude, wrong, broken, charged twice, will report).
- interested: they want the offer or to buy, or ask us to go ahead (yes, sounds good, how do I order).
- other: anything else, including thanks, emojis, out-of-office replies and text you cannot read.

If a reply both complains and asks to stop, choose stop.
