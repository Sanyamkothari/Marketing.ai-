WHAT IS IN THE FILE
-------------------
Customer purchase history over the past year (recency in months, a spend band, the dollars spent,
whether men's and women's merchandise were bought, an area type, whether the customer is new, the
purchase channel), the randomised e-mail group, and three outcomes over the two weeks after the
e-mail (a website visit, a conversion and the dollars spent). No names, addresses, e-mail addresses,
phone numbers or government identifiers; the file has no key at all, so `fetch.py` adds a row number.
