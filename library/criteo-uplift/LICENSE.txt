LICENCE — NON-COMMERCIAL
------------------------
Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).

This matters more than the other five licences in this library: **NonCommercial** means the
dataset may not be used in a commercial demo, a sales deck, a customer pilot or a shipped product.
ShareAlike means a derived dataset must carry the same licence. It is fine for internal research
and for the Phase 3b uplift work this dataset is reserved for; it is not fine for the demo script
in docs/LIBRARY.md, and that script does not list it.
